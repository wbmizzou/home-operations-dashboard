# Come back later

Ideas we've talked about and parked. Newest first.

## Cartoon house from a photo (parked Nov 22, 2026)
Let new households get a cute cartoon of *their* house, like the hand-drawn one for the original home.
- **Photo-guided picker (free, private):** upload a photo, tap the roof / siding / door to copy their real colors, pick the closest house shape. Runs on the phone; the photo never leaves it.
- **"Build your house" picker:** choose shape (ranch, split-level, two-story, bungalow, cabin, farmhouse), colors and extras (porch, shutters, garage, trees, fence). Free, no photo.
- **AI cartoon from a photo:** most magical, but needs an AI API key and costs a little per photo; delete the photo after drawing. Free tiers exist but have limits and may use uploads for training.
- **Manual:** friends send a photo and Claude draws it, like the original.

## Home Screen widget (parked Nov 2026)
iOS web apps can't provide real widgets.
- Free now: Shortcuts widget with buttons that open the app's pages.
- Live numbers: a Scriptable widget fed by a private, key-protected data link (counts only).
- Native: a Capacitor-wrapped app + Swift widget ($99/yr Apple Developer, needs a Mac).

## File attachments: receipts and photos (parked Nov 2026)
Attach receipts, invoices, permits and photos to projects (resale records). Needs a private storage bucket, short-lived links, 2-factor sign-in, and a "no sensitive ID documents" note. Don't store SSNs, bank/card numbers, IDs, tax or loan documents.

## Other ideas mentioned
- Home improvements report: printable/PDF list of completed projects with dates and costs for buyers.
- Reminders: push notifications when upkeep is due.
- Budget view: estimated vs quoted vs actual across projects.
- Real email sending (e.g. Resend) so sign-up confirmations never get rate-limited.
- Bot protection (CAPTCHA) on sign-up if spam appears.
- A short privacy note for people outside the household.
